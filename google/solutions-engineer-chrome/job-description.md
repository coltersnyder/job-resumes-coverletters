# About the job
In gTech Users and Products (gUP), our mission is to advocate for Google’s users by creating helpful and trusted experiences across the product ecosystem. We achieve this by meeting partners and consumers where they are with support and help, representing their needs with our product partners and proposing fixes and features that elevate their engagement with Google's diverse product ecosystem. Additionally we provide a range of product services that ensure our products are optimized for every user, no matter where they are in the world (e.g., localization, digitization, partner integration, and more).

The Chrome Scaled solutions team is part of the gTech Chrome and Web Ecosystem team supporting Chrome's vision of a healthy, vibrant web in this increasingly agentic world. We are a group of technical experts addressing the cross-product, incubation, activation and scale programs that are key to advancing the GTM goals of Chrome. By working with partners and developers, we aim to arm the ecosystem with the tools, incentives and products they need to succeed in the future of the web ecosystem.

As a Solutions Engineer on our team, you will be enabling Chrome to be the best browser for the Agentic AI era driving technical solutions, automation, data insights and technical needs for efficient operations across new and emerging product areas like Built-in AI, Agentic Web, Identity and Payments and help influence the trajectory of the entire web ecosystem by being the ecosystem.

Google creates products and services that make the world a better place, and gTech’s role is to help bring them to life. Our teams of trusted advisors support customers globally. Our solutions are rooted in our technical skill, product expertise, and a thorough understanding of our customers’ complex needs. Whether the answer is a bespoke solution to solve a unique problem, or a new tool that can scale across Google, everything we do aims to ensure our customers benefit from the full potential of Google products.
