\textbf{Re: Software Engineer III}

Dear Hiring Team,

I am a software engineer at Innovative Scientific Solutions, where I build simulation platforms that
model nuclear weapons effects for defense agencies. The work rewards two habits Google's engineering
culture is known for: measuring before optimizing, and treating correctness as a requirement rather
than a goal. I would like to bring those habits, and my M.S.\ in Computer Science from the Colorado
School of Mines, to a Software Engineer III role.

Performance is where I have taken the most initiative. Our team needed to benchmark its calculation
services at scale, so I spearheaded an internal Python tool, first as a command-line application and
then with a GUI front end, that runs any combination of our 13 effect calculations and charts the
results. It exposed a tenfold slowdown in how we call an external library, gave me clear graphs to
present the problem to leadership, and saved what would likely have been weeks of manual testing with
hand-written JSON inputs. I also port
legacy physics models from Fortran and Python to Rust without changing their numerical behavior, and
I build the Rust and Python back-end services, Docker deployments, and Angular front ends around them.
On our small agile team I lead development of individual features from requirements and design
documents through implementation and automated tests, and I review teammates' merge requests
regularly, since every change requires a separate reviewer and merger.

Debugging and test engineering have been constants in my work. As an intern at Tyler Technologies I
diagnosed a 2,000-test suite and restored about 1,500 tests to passing, so the team could rely on it
again. Data structures and algorithms are familiar ground as well: I co-taught CSCI 220, Data
Structures and Algorithms in C++, to 120 students, and I taught another section of 90. Explaining an
algorithm clearly enough for a room of undergraduates to implement it has made me a better reviewer
and a clearer writer of design documents.

I am most energized by problems where the details matter, and I would welcome the chance to bring that
to a team at Google. I live in Lakewood and would be glad to work from Google's Boulder office or remotely. Thank you for your time and consideration.

Sincerely,

Colter Snyder
